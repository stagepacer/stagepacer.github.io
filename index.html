StagePacer

A silent metronome for Apple Watch. The pulse is felt on the wrist, with an optional click, and keeps going while the screen is dark. An iPhone companion app sets tempo, meter, subdivision and pulse style, and starts or stops the Watch.

Support

Questions or problems: stagepacer@gmail.com.

Playback is limited to one hour per session by watchOS; press Start again for the next hour. Incoming calls and other system events can interrupt playback; StagePacer resumes when the system allows.
