StagePacer Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-09.

StagePacer is a personal metronome for Apple Watch with an iPhone companion app.

What StagePacer collects

Nothing. StagePacer has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software inside it. It never sends data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your devices

Your settings and presets (tempo, meter, subdivision, pulse style, sound, accents) are stored on your Apple Watch and iPhone. They move between those two devices only, through Apple's Watch Connectivity, so both show the same settings. Nothing about them leaves your devices.

StagePacer does not read or write health data and does not use location, contacts, photos, the microphone, or the camera.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction. StagePacer only learns whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock the Premium features on your devices.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
